# One-pager template

Keep these headings, in this order. Keep the whole page within the agreed length (default about 350 words).

```
# [Title: the subject and the ask, in one line]

**Assumptions:** audience: [...]; decision: [...]; length: [...]   <- include only when you could not ask the user

## Bottom line
[Two or three sentences: what is recommended or found, and what the reader is asked to do.]
Claim ("verbatim quote").

## Why
- [Key reason 1] ("verbatim quote")
- [Key reason 2] ("verbatim quote")
- [Key reason 3, at most five bullets in total] ("verbatim quote")

## Numbers that matter
| Figure | Value | Source quote |
|---|---|---|
| [what it measures] | [value with unit] | "verbatim quote" |

## Risks and open issues
- [What could go wrong, or what the source says it has not assessed] ("verbatim quote")
- [Anything in the source that corrects or undercuts the recommendation] ("verbatim quote")

## Next step
[The first action and its deadline, if the source gives them.] ("verbatim quote")
```

## Example of a good bullet

Good: Churn fell to 3.1 percent after the pricing change ("monthly churn was 3.1 percent in the second quarter").

Weak: Churn improved. (No figure, no quote, nothing the reader can check.)
